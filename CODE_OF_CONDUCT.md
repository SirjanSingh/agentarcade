# Code of conduct

agentarcade follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Read that for the full text. The short version:

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most people here are fitting this in around a
  day job.
- Critique code, not the person who wrote it.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome attention, in issues, PRs, discussions,
  or anywhere you are representing the project.
- Spam PRs (renamed variables, reworded READMEs, whitespace churn) get closed
  without review.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this privately, through the repository's
**Security** tab: **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with
`[conduct]`. Only the maintainer can read those reports. Please don't raise it
in a public issue.

The maintainer will reply, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban
anyone who breaks this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
